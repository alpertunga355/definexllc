01

Most pilots prepare the wrong way

Most pilots prepare the wrong way: they drill technical questions, memorise canned answers and try to sound impressive. Hiring boards are not looking for the smartest pilot in the building. They are looking for the person they can trust in a cockpit for the next twenty years.

That is a different skill set, and it is trainable.

Where the system came from

Built by a line pilot after failing an interview he was fully qualified for, then tested across seven airline interviews — US ultra-low-cost, US legacy and major international — with an offer from each.

02

Who it is for

If more than one of these is true, the programme is aimed at you.

  • You have the flight hours but not the interview confidence.
  • You are technically strong but present yourself unclearly.
  • You are moving across from military, international, cargo, charter or regional flying.
  • English is your second language and interview pressure adds load.
  • You want a repeatable system instead of forum and video-channel advice.

03

What is inside the book

  • A 90-second "tell me about yourself" structure.
  • A complete behavioural answer framework.
  • The real airline question categories, grouped.
  • Airline research protocol for the "why us?" question.
  • Interview-day stress management.
  • FAA / ICAO mindset differences for international applicants.
  • A 30-day preparation plan built around a working pilot roster.

05

What this is actually priced against

A single hire at a legacy carrier sets a seniority number, and a seniority number sets twenty years of pay, equipment and schedule.

That makes interview preparation a decision about career trajectory, not a book purchase. We price against the decision, and we say so rather than discounting into the floor.

Guarantee

Complete the workbook exercises and sit the mock board. If the debrief does not identify at least three specific score-killing blind spots in your answers, the coaching fee is refunded in full.
